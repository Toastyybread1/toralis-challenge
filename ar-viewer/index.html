Scale, privacy & what these markers mean

Coordinates are converted from Slicer millimetres to metres without enlarging the anatomy. AR is fixed at 100% scale; phone tracking is not a calibrated measurement tool.

Arrows illustrate direction, rings illustrate estimated radii, and B1, B2… label branch rows. They are not complete daughter-vessel surfaces. CT slices, patient identifiers and raw JSON are not shared.

Anyone with this unguessable QR link can view and potentially save the geometry. Access expires after one hour or when the publisher revokes it. Revocation cannot erase a snapshot already downloaded to a phone. Share only approved, de-identified demonstration data.
